Home Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Balaji Travels collects personal details such as Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, Passport numbers, and age lists during booking submissions or form enquiries to secure flight, rail, and hotel reservation slips.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your details are utilized strictly to issue ticketing records and visa filing applications with verified airlines, hoteliers, IRCTC systems, and embassy offices in Kolkata. We do not sell or trade your details to bulk advertising agencies.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Our platform utilizes cookies to enhance the user experience by storing active search categories and verifying consent guidelines. You can easily clear or disable tracking parameters directly in your browser.

4. Data Security

We enforce standard encryption policies to safeguard payment transactions and visa records. Direct database backups are audited regularly to eliminate any security loopholes or server leaks.
