## 1. Executive Summary & Growth Objectives

The goal of this Social Media Marketing (SMM / SMMM) blueprint is to establish **Balaji Travels** as the #1 most trusted, high-conversion travel agency and tour operator across Kolkata and Eastern India on **Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Google**.

### Key Growth Targets:
- **Lead Generation:** 150+ verified holiday and ticketing inquiries/month via direct WhatsApp clicks and form fills.
- **Conversion Rate:** Maintain a >15% lead-to-booking ratio through swift 15-minute response times.
- **Social Engagement:** Build a loyal community of 15,000+ Kolkata travelers on Facebook & Instagram.
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Optimize Meta Ad campaigns to generate qualified leads under ₹80 - ₹140 per lead.

## 2. Technical SMM Infrastructure (Implemented on Website)

The following conversion infrastructure has been directly coded into the website:

1. **Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Pixel Tracking:**
   - Base Pixel script initialized globally across all 15 pages.
   - Automatic `PageView` tracking on all visits.
   - Dedicated `Lead` event triggered automatically upon submission of both the Book Now modal and the Contact Page enquiry forms.
   - Custom `Contact` events tracked when visitors click the floating WhatsApp button or direct phone call links.

2. **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):**
   - Stream `G-HETP8B4C71` configured with `generate_lead` and `contact_click` events.

3. **Viral Social Sharing Engine (SMO):**
   - 1-Click WhatsApp & Social Share buttons directly embedded into every holiday tour package card and blog post.
   - Open Graph (OG) rich image previews customized with high-resolution destination graphics for Kashmir, Chardham Yatra, Maldives, Dubai, Australia, and Kerala.

WhatsApp is the highest-converting sales channel for Kolkata travelers.

### 1. Instant Automated Greeting:
> *"Namaste! 🙏 Welcome to Balaji Travels Kolkata. How can our travel desk assist you today?\n\n1️⃣ Holiday Tour Packages\n2️⃣ Flight Tickets (Domestic/International)\n3️⃣ IRCTC Train & Tatkal Booking\n4️⃣ Hotel & Resort Reservations\n5️⃣ Visa Assistance\n\nReply with a number to connect instantly with a travel advisor!"*

### 2. Follow-Up Lead Nurturing Protocol:
- **Within 5 Minutes:** Send personalized PDF itinerary and transparent price quotation.
- **After 24 Hours:** Share a traveler review / video from someone who recently completed that exact tour.
- **After 48 Hours:** Send a limited-period early bird discount: *"Book your seats before Friday to get free airport transfers or room upgrade."*
